Peach Cheesecake

A baked cheesecake on a brown-butter graham crust, finished with roasted peaches. Serves 12. About 90 minutes of work across an afternoon, plus an overnight chill you should not skip.

Ingredients

Crust

  • 200 g graham crackers (about 14 sheets), crushed fine
  • 85 g unsalted butter, browned and slightly cooled
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • ¼ tsp fine sea salt

Filling

  • 900 g full-fat cream cheese, at room temperature
  • 250 g granulated sugar
  • 240 g sour cream, at room temperature
  • 4 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • ¼ tsp fine sea salt

Roasted peach topping

  • 4 ripe peaches, halved, pitted, cut into eighths
  • 50 g light brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Brown the butter. Melt the butter over medium heat and keep it moving until the solids turn amber and smell nutty, 4–6 minutes. Cool five minutes.
  2. Make the crust. Stir the crumbs, sugar, and salt into the brown butter until it holds together when pressed. Pack it evenly into the bottom of a 23 cm springform pan. Bake at 175 °C (350 °F) for 10 minutes, then cool while you make the filling.
  3. Beat the cream cheese. Beat the cream cheese on medium until completely smooth, scraping the bowl twice. Lumps now are lumps forever. Add the sugar and beat two minutes more.
  4. Finish the filling. Beat in the sour cream, vanilla, lemon juice, flour, and salt. Add the eggs one at a time on low speed, mixing only until each disappears. Overbeating the eggs is how cracks happen.
  5. Bake gently. Wrap the pan's base in foil and set it in a roasting tray. Pour the filling over the crust, then pour hot water into the tray to come halfway up the pan. Bake at 160 °C (325 °F) for 60–70 minutes, until the edges are set and the centre still wobbles like set custard.
  6. Cool slowly. Turn the oven off, crack the door, and leave the cake inside for an hour. Then chill it, uncovered, overnight.
  7. Roast the peaches. Toss the peaches with the brown sugar, lemon juice, cinnamon, and salt. Roast at 200 °C (400 °F) for 15–20 minutes until soft and lightly caramelised. Cool completely.
  8. Serve. Release the springform, pile the peaches and their syrup over the top, and slice with a hot, dry knife.

Notes

  • Room-temperature dairy is the difference between smooth and grainy. Take everything out two hours ahead.
  • Frozen peach slices work for the topping; roast from frozen and add five minutes.
  • The cake keeps five days refrigerated. Add peaches per slice rather than all at once if serving over several days.
